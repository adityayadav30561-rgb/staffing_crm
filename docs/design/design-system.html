Principles

What makes the CRM screens feel clean. Keep these when designing new screens.

  • Calm surfaces, colour with a jobA white app on warm neutrals. Colour only marks meaning: a group of numbers, a status, an alert. Never decoration.
  • One action colourForest green with mint text means "do this" or "you are here": the primary button and the active nav item. Nothing else uses it.
  • Numbers firstFigures are large, in Mona Sans with tabular digits. Their labels are small and grey, so the eye lands on the number.
  • Three text levels onlyInk for content, ink-2 for supporting text, ink-3 for meta and captions. Hierarchy comes from size and grey, not from extra colours.
  • Hairlines, not boxesSections are split by 8% ink dividers and 10% rings. Shadows only on things that float (the app window, cards over photos).
  • Soft tint, dark textEvery tinted background pairs with a darker text of the same hue (mint-soft with forest, brass-soft with brown), so pills stay readable.
  • Rounded, but tightPills for actions, nav and status; 6 to 8px corners on cards and tiles; 16px on the app window. Small radii keep data dense.
  • Small, consistent iconsLucide icons at a 1.8 stroke, 12 to 16px, always next to a text label.
  • Believable sample dataIndian names, places and rupee figures, so screens read as real work. Always marked as sample data on the site.
  • Quiet motionSwitching views fades in over 180ms. No bounces or slides inside the app. Reduced-motion settings turn it off.

Typography

Two Google Fonts. Mona Sans (variable, with a width axis) is the display voice for headings and numbers; Funnel Sans is for body and all UI text. Every figure uses tabular digits.
