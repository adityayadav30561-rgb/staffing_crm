# Recruitment CRM — quick guide for the team

## Your day

1. **Dashboard**: open positions, this week's submissions, today's interviews, joinings in the next 7 days, and what's due. "My requirements" shows only requirements you own or are assigned to; "Whole team" shows everything.
2. **My tasks**: everything you need to chase. Most tasks are created for you by the CRM:
   - _Chase feedback_: the client hasn't replied N working days after you logged a submission (N is set per client, default 2).
   - _No client feedback for 5 working days_: goes to the requirement owner.
   - _Update outcome_: an interview ended two hours ago and nobody has logged what happened.
   - _Check in with …_: weekly while a selected candidate serves notice, daily in the last 3 days.
   - _Confirm joining_: on the expected joining date.
   - _Replacement guarantee ends_: a week before a placement's guarantee runs out.
     Tick a task when it's done. Reminders close by themselves when the situation is resolved (for example, when you record the client's feedback).

## A requirement from start to finish

1. **Client shares a position** → Requirements → New requirement. Paste the JD. It's saved as **Draft**.
2. Confirm the details and timeline with the client by phone, then **Change status → Confirmed — open**.
3. The owner or a manager assigns recruiters (Details & postings → Assign recruiters). They get a notification and a task.
4. Log where you posted the job (Details & postings → Job postings).
5. **Add candidate**: search the database first. If the person is new, add them under Candidates (a yellow box warns you if they might already exist).
6. Click the candidate's card to open the side panel and work through the stages:
   - **Sourced**: Contacted → Interested → _Move to Screening_.
   - **Screening**: fill the screening card (CTC, expected, notice, location, rating, remarks).
   - **Mark submitted to client**: send the profile by email or WhatsApp as usual, then log it here. Use _Client summary_ on the requirement to copy a ready-made table into your email.
   - **Submitted**: record the client's response (shortlisted, rejected with a reason, on hold).
   - **Interview**: log each round after you've coordinated it; tick _candidate confirmed_ and _panel confirmed_ for the line-up. Update the outcome after the interview. _Next round_ adds round 2, 3…
   - **Offer**: record offered CTC and expected joining date; Salary discussion → Offer released → Offer accepted.
   - **Joining**: flag a **joining risk** (counter-offer, other offer, not responding) if you sense trouble. Mark **Joined** on the day.
7. When all positions are filled, the CRM reminds you to confirm with the client and mark the requirement **Filled**.

You can drag cards between stages on the requirement's board. The CRM refuses moves that skip a step (for example, interview before the client shortlisted).

## Good to know

- The same candidate can be on several requirements; each has its own status. They can never be added twice to the same requirement.
- If the same client received the candidate in the last 90 days (for any role), you'll see a warning first.
- If someone changed a record after you opened it, the CRM tells you who and asks you to reload instead of overwriting their change.
- Your **referral link** is under your name (bottom left) → My profile. People who apply through it land in the database with you as the source.
- Managers see placements, fees and invoices, the full activity feed, and the audit history of every change.
